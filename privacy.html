Privacy Policy for GlancePanel

Last updated: August 20, 2026

GlancePanel is a local macOS utility. We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal data.

Data on your device

  • Screenshots and notes stay on your Mac.
  • Settings (folder bookmarks, preferences) are stored locally via macOS UserDefaults / security-scoped bookmarks.
  • Optional “Launch at login” uses Apple’s SMAppService; no data is sent to us.

No analytics, no accounts, no tracking, no third-party ads.
