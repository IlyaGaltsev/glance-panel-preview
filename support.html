Quick fixes

  • “App can’t be opened”

    GlancePanel is Developer ID signed and notarized. If macOS still blocks it: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

  • Panel doesn’t appear

    Needs a notched Apple Silicon MacBook and macOS 14.6+. Hover the black capsule under the notch (not the menu bar icons).

  • Screenshots empty

    Capture a new screenshot after installing, or check that your Screenshots folder is the default Desktop / Screenshots location.
